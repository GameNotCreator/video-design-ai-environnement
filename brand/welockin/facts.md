## What it does

- Cross-platform app and website blocker. Blocks apps, websites and notifications (site FAQ: "All of them").
- **Hard lock-in** (founder's word: "hardlockin"; the site calls it the **Nuclear lock** / Nuclear Mode): once a session
  starts, there is no way to turn it off before the timer ends. Site: "No pausing, no quitting, no bargaining your way out
  until the timer hits zero." FAQ: "With a Nuclear lock: yes [impossible to bypass]. It survives restarts and even
  uninstalling the app. Nothing turns it off before the timer or date you set."
- Strictness levels: from **Soft Lock** (pause or stop early allowed) to **Nuclear Lock** (pausing disabled, can't stop until
  the timer ends, survives restarts & uninstall). The help centre mentions "The five lock levels".
- Blocklists: bundle apps and sites (social, video, news, games) into reusable lists, launched in one tap.
- Sync: one session locks every connected device at once ("Block it once. Locked everywhere.").
- Scheduling (recurring focus blocks), timed breaks inside a session, focus sounds (rain, lo-fi, forest), password lock
  on settings.
- Set-up: "From install to locked-in in under a minute." Three steps: choose what to block, set it and lock in, select
  your devices.
