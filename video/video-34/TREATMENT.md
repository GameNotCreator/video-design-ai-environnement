# video-34 — Treatment: « Partiels » (FR), series « Deux trajectoires »

**Logline**: the same student on the same evening, twice, one floor above the other: upstairs he scrolls, downstairs
he locks his phone; exam day and grades show where each evening led.

**Why this format (stats of 25–26/11, `docs/performance-notes.md`)**
- The only TikTok breakout (Codex « Même nuit. Deux trajectoires », 5,830 views, 8.1 % likes) and the only shared
  video (Codex « closer », 19 shares) show what you GAIN by focusing; our loss stories plateau at ~500 views.
- The product is shown working (the lock card with its timer), as the client asked for more product explanation.
- Frame 0: two identical rooms, both scrolling, the hook written; the difference appears at 0.8 s (the lock).
- A new state every 1.6 s through the night (22:00, 23:30, 01:00), then exam day, then grades.
- Ends on a closed question (« Haut ou bas ? »), the easiest comment to write. In French; end card 3.2 s.
- Timely: French university exams (partiels) run December–January.

**Structures considered**: (a) side by side (left/right): too narrow in 9:16; (b) cutting between the two lives:
loses the direct comparison; (c) **two floors of a cut-away house** (chosen): the 9:16 frame splits naturally in two,
both lives are visible at every moment, and the gold-trimmed slab is the split line.

**Look**: a cut-away house at night in the hd-2d style (stars, stone foundation), two identical bedrooms (bed, window,
desk, lamp), then two exam halls (blackboard, a classmate). His red jumper is the only saturated colour. The lock card,
the marked papers and the clock are the only UI.
