# video-35 — Treatment: « Premier rendez-vous » (FR + EN), series « Deux trajectoires »

**Logline**: the same first date twice, one floor above the other: upstairs he scrolls and she leaves, downstairs he
locks his phone and they laugh; one year later, alone on a sofa or together on it.

**Why** (`docs/performance-notes.md`): the series shows what focus gives you (the only formats that broke out); a
living being to lose (her); the product shown working (the lock card, 0.8 s); frame 0 = the phone and the stake (her
across the table); a visible change every ≤ 2.5 s; ends on a closed question. Same cut-away house as video-34, so the
series reads at a glance.

**Structures considered**: (a) the date only, no time jump: no payoff; (b) a wedding at the end: too far, and a
cliché; (c) **one year later on the same sofa, alone or together, with his question** (chosen): the image carries the
result, the line is the joke people share.
