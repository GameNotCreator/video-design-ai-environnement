# video-3 — Treatment: "Life doesn't wait"

**Structures considered**: (1) one bench, one place, the friend's life walks past in seasons — *chosen*: the viewer sits
where he sits; (2) split screen phone feed vs. real life — too abstract at a glance; (3) chapter cards per life event —
cuts would break the feeling of time slipping past.
**Logline**: while he scrolls on a park bench, his best friend graduates, marries and has a son in front of him.
**Benchmark**: time-lapse "same place, years pass" shots; the style's RPG grammar (named NPC in a dialogue box). Learn
the rhythm, take nothing else.

**Music**: catalogue #2 "Can You Hear The Music" from track 0.5 s: its two big hits (19.00, 25.00) land on the look-up
and the end card; its quiet opening is lifted +4.6 dB under the montage. **Sound**: birds (spring/summer), wind (winter),

five season states; the hero in the red hoodie, beard after the wedding; the lamp is the only practical at the end.
