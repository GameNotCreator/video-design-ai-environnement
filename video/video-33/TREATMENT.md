# video-33 — Treatment: « Fais un vœu » (FR)

**Logline**: on his own birthday his friends sing, the candles burn down, the cake catches fire and everyone leaves;
he looks up from his phone and asks if it's his birthday.

**Why this script (stats of 25/11, `docs/performance-notes.md`, and the client's "no" to video-32)**
- video-32 (a ghost in a dark bedroom) was turned down: this one is warm, bright and needs no explanation.
- Frame 0 = the phone and the stake in one image: the cake with lit candles, four friends singing, him on his phone.
- The best retention comes from a new visible state every ≤ 2 s: here an escalation (candles melt 3.5, "Souffle !" 6.1,
  flare 7.5, fire 8.5, smoke and alarm 9.0, they stand 10.4, leave 11.6–14.5, he looks up 14.6).
- People to lose (his friends) and a reveal in the image (empty table, black cake), then a twist line people share.
- Sound from the first frame: everyone recognises "Joyeux anniversaire" at once; the track cuts the song on the flare.
- In French; end card 3.2 s.

**Structures considered**: (a) the friends wait and leave, no fire: too slow, nothing escalates; (b) a time-lapse of
several birthdays: done in video-31; (c) **one birthday that goes wrong step by step, ending on his question**
(chosen): each beat is bigger than the last, and the line turns the disaster into the joke.

**Look**: a dim dining room lit by the candles, a garland of muted pastel flags, two balloons, party cones on every head
(his is red: the only saturated accent). The fire is a source (the cake), so are the smoke and the alarm's red LED.
