# video-44 — Treatment: « Mamie » (FR + EN), series « Deux trajectoires »

**Logline**: Sunday, he promised to visit his grandmother, twice: upstairs he keeps scrolling while she waits with two
plates and a cake, downstairs he locks his phone and goes, and they bake it together.

**Why** (`docs/performance-notes.md`): the rule « un être vivant qui attend » (someone waiting is the strongest stake we
have measured), series format, the product at 0.8 s, frame 0 = the phone + the written hook, a visible change every
≤ 2 s (he leaves; plates, the window, the clock, the oven, the cake), the reveal in the image (she is alone at the table
/ they laugh over the cake), a closed question at the end. First episode where the two floors show the person waiting.

**Structures considered**: (a) his room only, with her messages on his screen: text-driven; (b) a family dinner:
too many people; (c) **his room for the hook, then her kitchen on both floors, the same afternoon** (chosen).
