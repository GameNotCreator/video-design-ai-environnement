# video-2 — Treatment: "Five more minutes"

## Three candidate structures

1. **A day-cycle map that becomes a life (chosen).** One set, one bedroom diorama, one continuous camera move. The
   window runs through days, nights and seasons faster and faster while the boy on the bed grows old. Everything in
   the room changes except the cold light of the phone on his face.
2. **Chapter cards per decade** ("AGE 20", "AGE 40"…): RPG grammar, clear, but cuts break the feeling of a life slipping
   by in one breath.
3. **Mirror rhyme**: open and close on the same framing, young then old. Strong, but needs time we don't have in a
   TikTok; kept only as the camera's return to the opening framing at the end.

## Logline and arc

A student says "five more minutes" and doesn't look up for sixty-five years.
Setup (age 19, five more minutes) → turn (time runs away; the room fills with years) → ending (he dies holding the
phone; it falls, still glowing; a notification nobody reads; the screen goes dark).

## Benchmark

- Learn from: time-lapse "life in one room" shorts (the room as clock: light, seasons, clutter), and the style's own
  *day-cycle map* variation. Don't take: any film's shots, characters, music cues or text.

## Look

- Dollhouse cut-away bedroom (two walls, plank floor), high 3/4 view, tilt-shift band on the bed.
- Two temperatures: the window light (warm day / blue night) sweeps the room on the time-lapse; the phone's cold blue
  glow is the one constant practical, on his face, every frame.
- The hero wears the red hoodie (`#C41919`), the only saturated colour. He ages through 6 drawings (19, 27, 38, 52, 66,
  79): stubble, beard, glasses, grey, bald, white, a growing hunch. Pose changes are instant cuts (style rule).
- The room keeps the score: wall calendar year 2026 → 2091, pizza boxes and cans pile up, the plant on the desk dies,
  cobwebs; tree outside cycles through seasons.

## Beats (≤ 8 lines)

```
0.0–2.8   Afternoon. Age 19 on his bed, scrolling. Box types: "Just five more minutes."
2.8–4.6   Window light starts sliding; "AGE 19" counter appears; the first night falls.
4.6–15.0  Drop: days strobe by, seasons turn, years climb 19 → 84, clutter piles up, he ages; phone light never changes.
15.0–15.8 Last night. Old man, head nodding. Music stops dead on a downbeat (15.76): he slumps.
15.8–17.6 The phone slips, falls to the floorboards, still glowing. A notification. Nobody. Screen goes dark.
18.0–23.0 "Stop scrolling." / "Do something with your life."
23.0–28.4 Cream card: logo · "WeLockIn helps you focus on what matters." · "Available on iOS · Windows · macOS".
```

## Cue map

Track: **"you lost - Scheming (Instrumental Slowed)"** (catalogue #6, 86 BPM, sad/atmospheric). Chosen because its
quiet intro and its bass entry at 14.40 s give exactly the "life starts slipping" moment, and its mood is the film's.
- Film 0.0 = track 9.8 (quiet bed under the hook).
- Track bass entry 14.40 → film **4.6** (time starts racing).
- Hard stop on the downbeat track 25.56 → film **15.76** (death), short low-passed reverb tail, then silence.
- Film 18.0–28.4: track intro 0.0–10.4 under the text and end card, at −9 dB, fade out.
