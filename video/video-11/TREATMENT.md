written; one location; three beats of escalation; the turn before 70 % of the running time; ironic punchline; ~20 s.

**Music**: "I'll Do It (Instrumental)" (lucii. - Topic). track 6.0 s so its rise (track 17 s) lands on the waiter's "Yes." (film 11.0), hard stop on the bill; track 16.6 s under the end card.

the format is recognisable from the first frame.
