# video-32 — Treatment: « Futur toi » (FR)

**Logline**: the ghost of himself at 80 begs him to look up and fades with every regret; he turns the volume up, and
when he finally takes an earbud out, the room is empty.

**Why this script (stats of 25/11, `docs/performance-notes.md`)**
- The best TikTok hold is Mirror (74 % at 1 s, 51 % at 2 s): himself + a surreal twist. Here, himself at 80.
- Frame 0 = the phone and the stake in one image: him lit by the phone, the old ghost shaking him, the hook written.
- The reveal is seen, not read: the ghost loses opacity with each regret, then is gone.
- No lull after the hook: a new visible state every ≤ 2.8 s (shout 3.0, volume 4.0, regrets 5.6 / 8.5 / 11.3, each
  in a new place with a flicker, fade 13.6, laugh 14.2, earbud 15.1).
- In French (TikTok audience 80–98 % French-speaking), end card 3.2 s (test).

**Structures considered**: (a) the ghost only shouts and he never reacts: one note, no escalation; (b) the ghost
writes on the fogged window: text-driven, which underperforms; (c) **three regrets, each fainter, then gone, then his
question** (chosen): the fading is a clock you can see, and the last line ("…t'as dit un truc ?") is the joke people
share.

**Look**: his bedroom at night (cold blue, moon in the window), posters of Tokyo and a mountain (what he never does).
The ghost: the same pixel character aged (white hair, beard, hunched), tinted cold, see-through, a faint glow; he
jumps from spot to spot with a flicker. The volume pop-up is the only UI. His red jumper is the only saturated colour.
