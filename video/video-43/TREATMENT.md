# video-43 — Treatment: « Le chien » (FR + EN), series « Deux trajectoires »

**Logline**: his dog waits for its evening walk, leash in its mouth, twice: upstairs he scrolls and the dog gives up by
the door, downstairs he locks his phone and they go out; six months later his sister notices the difference.

**Why** (`docs/performance-notes.md`): the rule « un être vivant qui attend » (Fetch, the dog, was one of the better
emotional videos), series format, the product at 0.8 s, frame 0 = the phone + the dog with the leash + the written hook,
a visible change every ≤ 2 s (out the door, back, the ball, asleep together / lying down, by the door, asleep alone),
the reveal in the image (a chubby sad dog / a bouncing one), a light joke rather than a tragedy (« …il est juste
poilu »), a closed question at the end. Different from Fetch: no years passing, no loss, the same evening repeated.

**Structures considered**: (a) Fetch again, a whole life: done; (b) a vet visit: a new set for one line; (c) **the
evening on two floors, then the same visit six months later** (chosen).
