downstairs he locks his phone and cleans; on Monday night his flatmate walks out upstairs and cooks for him downstairs.

the pizza box, the cans, the lights), the reveal in the image (she walks out / she cooks), a closed question at the end.

(c) **Sunday night on two floors, then the same Monday-night homecoming** (chosen).
