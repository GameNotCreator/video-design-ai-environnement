# video-41 — Treatment: « Le sommeil » (FR + EN), series « Deux trajectoires »

**Logline**: the night before an 8 a.m. class, twice: upstairs he scrolls until 3, downstairs he locks his phone at 11
and sleeps; in the lecture hall one dozes in his seat, the other stands with the answer.

**Why** (`docs/performance-notes.md`): series format, the product shown at 0.8 s, frame 0 = the phone in both rooms +
the written hook, the night counted on the clock (00:00, 01:30, 03:00), the reveal in the image (asleep / hand up), a
closed question at the end. The lecture hall reuses the demo-day set of video-40.

**Structures considered**: (a) the morning alarm only: no contrast; (b) an exam: done in « Partiels »;
(c) **the night on two floors, then the same lecture hall, the same question** (chosen).
