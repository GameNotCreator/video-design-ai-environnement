# video-31 — Treatment: « Premiers pas » (FR)

**Logline**: his daughter walks to him for the first time while he scrolls; eighteen years pass in four jumps; when he
finally looks up, he asks if she can walk yet.

**Why this script (stats of 25/11, `docs/performance-notes.md`)**
- TikTok audience 80–98 % French-speaking → first video in French (client OK in this request).
- Frame 0 = the phone AND the stake in one image (best TikTok hold: Mirror 74 % at 1 s; Surgeon on YouTube): the baby
  standing at the table, him lit by the phone, the mother's open arms, the hook written.
- A living being to lose and an emotional reveal shown in the image (Fetch, Mirror > gags): she grows up and leaves;
  he is grey and alone.
- No lull after the hook (YouTube cliff at 4.5–7 s): a new visible state every ≤ 2 s (steps 0.4–2.8, "Pa… pa ?" 3.0,
  turn 4.2, hug 5.4, jumps 5.7 / 8.5 / 11.7, she leaves 10.5, door 13.0–14.2, he looks up 14.4).
- Shorter end card (test): 3.2 s instead of ~4.2 s.

**Structures considered**: (a) the baby walks past him to the mother and the film ends there: strong but no time,
no payoff; (b) a height chart on the door frame fills up: label-driven, which underperforms; (c) **four ages, one line
each, ending on his question** (chosen): every jump is a visible state (her size, his beard, the window's light), and
the last line turns the sadness into the joke that makes people share it.

**Look**: the living room in the hd-2d style; the room in depth (baby in the foreground, him on the sofa at the back)
because the 9:16 frame is narrow. Her purple at every age is the continuity cue; his red is the only saturated accent.
Close push on his face when he looks up (≥ 1/4 of the height).
