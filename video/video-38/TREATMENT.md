# video-38 — Treatment: « Erasmus » (FR + EN), series « Deux trajectoires »

**Logline**: six months before a semester in Madrid, twice: upstairs he scrolls, downstairs he locks his phone and
learns Spanish; at a Madrid café one points at the menu and ends up alone with his phone, the other orders and the girl
at the next table comes over.

**Why** (`docs/performance-notes.md`): series format (what focus gives you), the product shown at 0.8 s, frame 0 =
the phone in both rooms + the written hook, a visible change every ≤ 2 s (flashcards cover the wall, earbuds, the
suitcase empty / packed, the dark), the reveal in the image (alone with the phone / three at one table), ends on a
closed question. "La salle de sport" moved down the backlog: the rule "objet ou système" says such concepts do worse;
Erasmus has people reacting to him, like episodes 2 and 4.

**Structures considered**: (a) Madrid only, lost in the city: no cause; (b) a language-app style montage: off-product
(WeLockIn does not teach languages, he learns by himself); (c) **the six months on two floors, then the same café
terrace, the same waiter's question** (chosen).
