# video-40 — Treatment: « Le projet » (FR + EN), series « Deux trajectoires »

**Logline**: the same app idea on a sticky note, twice: upstairs he scrolls for a year, downstairs he locks his phone
and builds it; at the demo day the screen lights up with that app and someone walks on stage: upstairs his friend
("…that was my idea."), downstairs him.

**Why** (`docs/performance-notes.md`): series format, the product shown at 0.8 s, frame 0 = the phone in both rooms +
the note + the written hook, a visible change every ≤ 2 s (laptop code, notes multiply, the dark, asleep), the reveal
in the image (the same sketch on the big screen; who walks on stage), a relatable sting ("someone else built your
idea"), ends on a closed question. Roles swapped on the same set, as in « L'été » (video-37).

**Structures considered**: (a) the launch on a phone screen: small, text-driven; (b) a crowd of downloads: abstract;
(c) **the year on two floors, then the same demo day, the same "and the award goes to…"** (chosen).
