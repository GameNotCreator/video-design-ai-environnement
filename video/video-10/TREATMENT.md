# video-10 — Treatment: "Imagine if… #3: the lifeguard"

Built on the lessons of `docs/performance-notes.md`: frame 0 shows the whole situation with its hook text already
written; one location; three beats of escalation; the turn before 70 % of the running time; ironic punchline; ~20 s.

**Beats**: frame 0 : la plage, le maître-nageur sur sa chaise + « IMAGINE IF · EP. 3 / Don't worry. Someone's watching. » · au loin SWIMMER « HELP! » · LIFEGUARD « One sec. » · gros plan : elle tient son téléphone en live (point rouge) « Wait… I'm live. » · elle coule, seul le bras et le téléphone dépassent, bulles · noir · « Imagine if they scrolled like you do. » · carte finale.

**Music**: "Stranger Things Main Theme" (L'Orchestra Cinématique). track 0.8 s under the beach (series theme), stop with a short tail as she sinks, then 0.8 s again under the end card.
**Sound**: waves, gulls, splashing, live ping, bubbles.
**Series** (#1–#3): same caption grammar ("IMAGINE IF · EP. N" + a reassuring line), same theme, same punchline, so
the format is recognisable from the first frame.
