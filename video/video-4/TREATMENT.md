# video-4 — Treatment: "Next Christmas"

**Structures considered**: (1) the same Christmas dinner four times, one chair empties — *chosen*: repetition makes the
absence land; (2) one dinner where people fade out one by one — less clear without time markers; (3) grandma's side of
the story — loses the viewer's point of view.
**Logline**: every Christmas his grandma asks him something; every Christmas he answers his phone; the last one, her
place is empty.
**Benchmark**: holiday ads built on a repeated scene; the style's chapter-card grammar (a year label per dinner).
